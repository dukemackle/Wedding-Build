# Guest-site art: ChatGPT prompts (2026-10-29)

The owner's brief: the current art doesn't fit the templates. It looks like
stickers, and some of it isn't pretty. Every piece below is drawn for one
template and one spot on its page.

## Why the old art looks like stickers, and how these prompts fix it

1. **It floats.** A cut-out sprig sits in the middle of nowhere. New pieces
   **run off the edge of the canvas**, so they look like part of the page,
   cropped from a bigger painting.
2. **It wasn't drawn for the layout.** New pieces are shaped for their spot
   (an arch that wraps the arched photo, a corner swag, a horizon strip) and
   **leave empty space** where the names and photos go.
3. **Mixed artists.** Each template keeps **one style** for all its pieces.

## How to use them

- Use **ChatGPT** (image generation). It can make real transparent PNGs; Grok can't.
- **Start one chat per template** and keep the whole template in that chat. After
  the first image, begin each prompt with *"Same artist, same style, same
  palette as the previous image."* That keeps a set consistent.
- **Paste the template's Style line, then the piece prompt, then the Rules
  block** (bottom of this file).
- Ask for the size given (ChatGPT offers 1024×1024, 1536×1024 wide, 1024×1536
  tall). Regenerate freely; only keep a piece you'd frame.
- Download as PNG and name it as given (e.g. `heirloom-arch.png`), then send
  them to Claude. Claude trims, converts and wires them in, then previews them
  on the real page at phone and desktop sizes before anything ships.
- **Start with Heirloom.** It already uses art and it's the template most
  judged on beauty. If that set works, do the rest.

## 1. Heirloom: formal invitation, arched photo, ivory/sage or dusty blue

**Style:** Loose, luminous watercolour in the manner of a high-end stationery
studio. Garden roses, ranunculus, sweet peas and trailing jasmine vine with
olive and eucalyptus leaves. Palette: ivory, blush, soft peach, dusty sage, a
touch of muted dusty blue. Delicate wet-on-wet edges, visible brush texture,
light and airy, never heavy or saturated.

- **heirloom-arch.png** (1024×1536 tall): A floral garland shaped as an arch,
  following the curve of a tall arched window. Flowers cluster at the top
  shoulders and trail down both sides, getting thinner towards the bottom.
  The inside of the arch is completely empty and transparent (a photo goes
  there). The arch is open at the bottom.
- **heirloom-corner.png** (1024×1024): A corner swag of roses and trailing
  vine coming in from the top-left corner, running off the top and left
  edges of the canvas. It fills only the top-left third; the rest is empty.
- **heirloom-ledge.png** (1536×1024 wide): A low, wide horizontal spray of
  flowers and leaves along the bottom of the canvas, densest in the centre
  and tapering to fine tendrils at both ends, like flowers laid on a table
  beneath an invitation. Top two-thirds empty.
- **heirloom-divider.png** (1536×1024 wide): A very slim, delicate horizontal
  sprig, a single rosebud with leaves either side, in the middle of the
  canvas. Thin, used between sections.

## 2. Riviera: Amalfi postcard, blue and lemon, colour bands

**Style:** Bold, sunny Mediterranean gouache. Flat confident brushstrokes,
like a vintage Italian travel poster painted by hand. Lemons, glossy lemon
leaves, white blossom, bougainvillea. Palette: lemon yellow, warm gold, deep
royal blue, sky blue, white, a little leaf green. Crisp, cheerful, slightly
graphic.

- **riviera-branch.png** (1024×1024): A lemon branch with fruit, leaves and a
  few white blossoms, reaching in from the top-right corner and running off
  the top and right edges. Fills the top-right third only.
- **riviera-coast-wide.png** (1536×1024 wide): A painted coastline: pastel
  cliffside village stepping down to a deep blue sea, a striped beach
  umbrella, a small boat. The scene fills the bottom half; the sky fades to
  plain transparent at the top so names can sit over it. *(Not transparent
  overall. Ask for the sky to fade out.)*
- **riviera-coast-tall.png** (1024×1536 tall): The same coast composed for a
  phone screen: village and sea in the bottom 40%, sky fading out above.
- **riviera-tile.png** (1024×1024): A single square majolica tile, blue and
  yellow, lemons and a geometric border, painted. Edge to edge (it will
  repeat as a border).

## 3. Night Swim: dark navy and gold, names under the stars

warm brown ink.

- **homestead-landscape-wide.png** (1536×1024 wide): Rolling hills and distant

- **homestead-landscape-tall.png** (1024×1536 tall): The same scene composed

- **homestead-desert-wide.png** (1536×1024 wide): The same style but a desert

- **homestead-grasses.png** (1024×1536 tall): A tall bunch of wild grasses,

- **homestead-divider.png** (1536×1024 wide): A slim divider: a single wheat

- **classic-divider.png** (1536×1024 wide): A slim horizontal sprig of olive

## 7. Monogram: minimal and modern, initials huge

**Style:** One continuous unbroken line, modern single-line drawing. Black
line only on transparent. Sparse, elegant, lots of air.

- **monogram-stem.png** (1024×1536 tall): A single long-stemmed flower (calla

## 8. Editorial: magazine cover, black/ivory or burgundy

**Style:** A single bold ink-brush gesture, like a sumi-e brushstroke in a
fashion magazine. Black ink only on transparent. Dramatic, confident, minimal.

- **editorial-stem.png** (1024×1536 tall): One oversized brush-painted
  stem with a single bloom, entering from the right edge and running off it.

## Scenery pack (any template, optional)

Full painted views for couples whose venue is a mountain, coast or vineyard.
These need a new "Background scene" option built in the editor. Do these
after the template sets.

**Style:** Airy editorial watercolour landscape, soft and luminous, no people,
no buildings unless named. The top half fades to plain white so names sit on
it. Make a wide (1536×1024) and a tall (1024×1536) version of each.

- **scene-mountains**: Snow-capped mountain range, pine forest and an alpine
  lake in the foreground, morning light.
- **scene-coast**: Rocky headland, a sweep of beach and sea, soft surf.
- **scene-vineyard**: Rows of vines over rolling hills, cypress trees, late sun.
- **scene-desert**: Red rock mesas, desert bloom, warm evening sky.
- **scene-lake**: A still lake ringed by forest, a wooden dock.
- **scene-city**: A painted city skyline at dusk, loose and impressionistic.

## Rules block (paste at the end of every prompt)

> Transparent background (real PNG alpha), unless this prompt says otherwise.
> No paper texture, no background colour, no drop shadow, no border or frame,
> no text, no lettering, no signature, no watermark. Where the prompt says the
> art runs off an edge, let it be cut off by that canvas edge as if cropped
> from a larger painting. Keep every empty area truly empty. Elegant,
> restrained, high-end wedding stationery quality, high detail. Original art,
> not in the style of any named living artist.
