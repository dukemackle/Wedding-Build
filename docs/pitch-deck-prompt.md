**3. The problem / why.**
- Planning a wedding means juggling a dozen tools: a spreadsheet for the budget, another for guests, a separate website, a directory for vendors, plus endless email threads.
- Even with The Knot or Zola, the common advice is "use their app *plus* a spreadsheet for your budget", because their budget tools are an afterthought. For them, planning tools are a funnel to sell vendor ads or registry purchases.
- "Free" isn't really free: big platforms make money from registry cuts and from charging vendors $200–$400+/month, and couples pay in ads and upsells (e.g., ~$15 for seating charts, ~$80 for guest texting).
- Couples' biggest anxieties are money, the guest list, and the day itself.

**4. Product — features and how a couple uses it.** Show it as a simple journey (Sign up → enter date, location, guest count, style → get a real budget → build guest list → find venues/vendors → seat everyone → run the day). Features (the 10 dashboard tiles):
- **Budget**: real cost estimates for your region, season and style, plus every quote, payment, due date and who's paying (e.g., parents' split). Deeper than competitors'.
- **Guests**: RSVPs, meals, addresses and plus-ones in one bulk-editable table. A Contact Collector link lets guests enter their own addresses.
- **Venues**: browse on a map (Zillow-style), shortlist and send inquiries.
- **Vendors**: every vendor conversation and quote in one place, with Wren drafting inquiry emails.
- **Checklist**: a month-by-month plan built around your date.
- **Bookings**: everyone you've booked, with contracts and contacts.
- **Attire**: dresses, suits and rings. Save favorites, then buy or rent.
- **Itinerary**: a printable hour-by-hour run sheet for the day.
- **Venue Layout**: drag tables into your room and seat everyone, with a printable seating chart and a "find your table" sign (free; Zola charges ~$15 for this).
- **Wedding website**: free, in your own style, with RSVPs, travel/hotels, dress code, FAQ, a photo gallery and a guest photo wall via table-card QR codes.
- **Wren AI**: drafts vendor emails and thank-you notes from the gift log.
