# Paid advertising

**Owns:** paid search, paid social and sponsored placements, when the owner
decides to spend.

**Status:** blocked. No analytics or conversion tracking to measure ads, no
disclosed pixels, no budget. Do not propose spend until `analytics.md` step 1
is live and the owner has set a test budget.

**Objectives (when unblocked):** cost per activated couple (not per click or
signup), and cost per claimed listing for B2B tests. Every test has a cap and
a stop rule set before it starts.

## SOP (when unblocked)

1. Start with the cheapest high-intent test: Google Search on a handful of
   exact local queries landing on the estimator or a metro venues page. Small
   fixed budget, two weeks, one metro.
2. Paid social (Pinterest/Meta) only after organic content shows which
   messages get saves. Ads that use the Meta/Pinterest pixel need the Privacy
   update and, for EU traffic, consent. Prefer server-side conversion APIs
   only after the legal doc says so.
3. Ad copy follows compliance: no "best", "#1", "verified vendors", no prices or
   counts that aren't current, no implied endorsements.
4. Never target by sensitive categories; don't upload customer lists to ad
   platforms without a privacy review.
5. Report: spend, clicks, signups, activated couples, cost per activated
   couple, and the decision (stop/continue/scale) to DECISIONS.md.
