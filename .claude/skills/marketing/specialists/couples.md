# Couple acquisition and growth

**Owns:** getting engaged couples to sign up and start planning.

**Objectives:** signups per week (from `/admin/growth`, test accounts
excluded); activation = a couple completes `/start` and uses one core tool
(budget, guests or site) within 7 days; source mix once tracking exists.

**Inputs:** STATE.md "Verified claims", `docs/competitors.md` (where we win:
the budget tool, actually free, spreadsheet replacement, free extras others
charge for), research findings.
**Outputs:** positioning and landing copy, channel bets with a test plan,
free-tool ideas, launch plan, drafts for social/SEO/partners to execute.

## SOP

1. Start from the wedge, not from features: "actually free" and "a budget that
   beats your spreadsheet" are the honest, differentiated claims. The free
   estimator at `/estimate` (no signup) is the front door.
2. Every channel bet gets: audience, message, the one page it lands on, the
   metric, a cost cap in owner hours and dollars, and a stop rule. Log in
   `EXPERIMENTS.md`.
3. Organic first (SEO, Pinterest, Reddit participation by the owner, guest-site
   word of mouth); paid only after analytics exists.
4. **The guest site is the built-in growth loop.** Every couple's site is seen
   by ~100+ guests, many of them future couples. A tasteful, removable
   "Made with You Do, I Do" footer link is the cheapest acquisition channel we
   have (not built). It's a visual change → `/design-expert` and a preview.
5. Landing changes must keep the dashboard-preview rule in CLAUDE.md.

**Hypotheses worth testing (not facts):** couples search "wedding budget
calculator [state]" and similar; engagement season (Dec–Feb) is when most
sign up for planning tools; guest-site footers drive signups.

**Collaborates with:** SEO (landing and estimator pages), social (content),
analytics (attribution), lifecycle (activation after signup).
