# Social media and content

**Owns:** what we publish: social posts, short video scripts, Pinterest pins,
blog/guide content (with SEO), and visual assets for them.

**Objectives:** a sustainable cadence the owner can actually keep (start: 3
posts a week on one platform); saves and clicks to the landing or estimator,
not likes; content that's reusable across platforms.

**Inputs:** STATE.md verified claims, research on what couples search and
save, real screenshots of the product, `/wren-voice`, brand colours in
CLAUDE.md. **Outputs:** a monthly content calendar and ready-to-post drafts in
`drafts/` (copy, alt text, the screenshot or asset brief, hashtags, the link
with UTM tags from `analytics.md`).

## SOP

1. **One platform first.** Hypothesis: Pinterest (planning-intent, long shelf
   life, links out) then Instagram/TikTok. Confirm with research before
   committing; record the choice in DECISIONS.md.
2. **Content pillars** (adjust after results): budget truth (real cost
   ranges from our estimator, with the region and source), planning
   checklists and timelines, guest-site and seating how-tos, "what nobody
   tells you" wedding logistics, local venue spotlights (only facts from the
   listing; no "recommended").
3. Show the real product. Screenshots from the app, not mock-ups of features
   that don't exist. No stock "happy couple" claims about our users.
4. Never use a couple's guest site, photos, names or wedding details without
   their written permission, recorded in DECISIONS.md.
5. Images of venues/vendors: only ones we have rights to (their own uploads
   on a claimed listing with permission, or our own). Credit as required.
6. The owner posts. Drafts carry status `draft` until approved.

**Brand:** wordmark "You do, *I do*"; navy `#14203d` and Sunshine `#e0a100`
on existing screens, the 2026-09-28 palette for anything new (CLAUDE.md
contrast rules apply to graphics too). Wren (the bird) speaks for the
assistant, not the company. Calm, warm, a little playful, never stressful.
