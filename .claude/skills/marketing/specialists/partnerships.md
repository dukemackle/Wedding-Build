# Partnerships and referrals

**Owns:** other people's audiences: planners, wedding bloggers and podcasts,
venue groups, wedding shows, bridal shops, officiants, local directories,
and couple-to-couple and vendor-to-couple referral.

**Objectives:** partner-sourced signups and claims (needs `ref`/UTM capture
from `analytics.md`); number of live partner arrangements; owner hours spent.

## What exists

Each wedding has a referral code (`WREN-XXXXXX`, migration 0040) used only to
tag vendor inquiries; the Terms call it "informal tracking only". No referral
rewards, partner page or affiliate links. `docs/monetization.md` calls a
self-built referral system weak as revenue infrastructure. Respect that.

## SOP

1. **Give-first partnerships that cost nothing:** a vendor who lists with us
   can link couples to our free planning tools; a planner can use the budget
   tool with clients; a blogger can embed or link the estimator. Pitch the
   tool's usefulness, never a revenue share we haven't decided.
2. **Vendor-to-couple referral is the natural loop.** A claimed vendor tells
   their booked couples "plan the rest here, free". Draft the one-line
   message and a `?ref=` link once source capture exists.
3. **Couple referral rewards:** don't build until there are real users and the
   owner decides what a reward is (judgement call, monetization-adjacent).
4. **Pitches:** drafts only, personal, one per partner, with what's in it for
   them. Partner pitches are commercial email → the CAN-SPAM gate applies to
   cold email; a reply to someone who wrote to us first is fine.
5. Never name anyone as a partner publicly without a written yes, recorded in
   DECISIONS.md. Never accept payment for placement in Phase 0.
6. Before any formal agreement, revenue share or co-branding: owner +
   attorney (entity is still open, legal-audit R4).
