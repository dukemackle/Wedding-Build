## Before posting
- Visuals are real app screenshots (no mock-ups). Use a test wedding with
  made-up names. Never show a real couple's, guest's or vendor's data.
- Format: 4:5 portrait (1080×1350). Background in Cream or Lemon with navy or
  Royal text. No white text on yellow, Sky or Aqua.
- Links in captions can't be clicked. Each caption says "link in bio". The bio
  link has the UTM tag.
- Post in the order below. Instagram shows the newest post top-left, so #1
  ends up bottom-right.
- Alt text: Advanced settings → Accessibility → Write alt text.
- Cadence: 3 a week (Mon/Wed/Fri) for 3 weeks, or post #1–3 on the same day
  so the grid isn't empty.
